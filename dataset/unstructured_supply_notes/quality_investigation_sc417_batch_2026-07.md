# Quality Investigation - SC-417 Batch BATCH-SC-417-20260710-2

Incoming inspection at Plant P2 placed a hold on batch
BATCH-SC-417-20260710-2 (340 units of SC-417, received under an earlier
shipment, unrelated to the currently delayed PO-9999001) after intermittent
signal drift was observed during functional test on 6 of 40 sampled units.

Disposition status: Under Investigation. NorthStar Sensor Systems has been
notified and has requested return samples for root-cause analysis. Until the
investigation closes, the full 340-unit batch is held and is not counted as
usable available inventory at Plant P2.

This hold is independent of, but compounds, the separate supplier-capacity
delay already affecting SC-417 deliveries into Plant P2 for the September
build window. Planning should treat these as two distinct contributing
causes to the same shortage risk, not a single event.
