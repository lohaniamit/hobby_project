# S&OP Planning Meeting Summary - Harvester X9 1200 Series (HRV-03)

North America demand planning raised the September forecast for the
Harvester X9 1200 Series from the prior baseline to reflect stronger
dealer order momentum ahead of the fall harvest season. Several dealers in
the Corn Belt submitted early commitment orders in late July, ahead of
normal seasonal patterns.

The revised forecast (see demand_forecast.csv, forecast_type = "Uplift",
period 2026-09) increases planned demand for HRV-03 in North America by
roughly a third versus the prior baseline. Plant P2's September 11 build
slot for HRV-03 (production plan PLAN-9999001) was already scheduled before
this uplift was approved, and the plan is now flagged At Risk given known
constraints on the SC-417 sensor module.

Action: Materials Planning to confirm whether the SC-417 supply position at
Plant P2 can support the original build quantity, the uplift, or requires a
phased release.
