# Supplier Risk Note - NorthStar Sensor Systems (SUP-00042)

NorthStar Sensor Systems, our single approved source for the SC-417 Grain Flow
Sensor Module used on the Harvester X9 1200-series platform, has flagged a
capacity constraint for the July-October production window.

Per the supplier's weekly capacity call on 2026-07-16, NorthStar reports that
an upstream semiconductor allocation issue at one of their tier-2 component
vendors is reducing usable board output. Committed volume for SC-417 already
exceeds the revised capacity figure for August and September.

Purchase order PO-9999001 (600 units, promised 2026-08-20, destination Plant
P2) is now tracking late. NorthStar's account team has verbally committed to
expedite partial shipment but has not confirmed a firm recovery date in
writing as of this note.

Recommend: (1) escalate to NorthStar's VP of Operations, (2) evaluate the
approved SC-418 substitute for near-term coverage, (3) check alternate-plant
inventory for a possible stock transfer while the PO is outstanding.

Risk rating on file for this supplier has been raised to Medium-High pending
resolution.
