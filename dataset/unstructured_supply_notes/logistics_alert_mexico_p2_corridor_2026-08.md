# Logistics Alert - Mexico to Plant P2 Truck Corridor, August 2026

Elevated congestion and border-crossing delays are affecting truck freight
on the Mexico-to-Plant-P2 corridor (lane LANE-000966) throughout August
2026. Average lead time on this lane has increased versus the prior
quarter, and the lane's risk score has been raised from Medium to High for
the August period (see logistics_lanes.csv).

Shipments already in transit on this lane, including SHP-0021122 carrying
PO-9999001 (SC-417, destined for Plant P2), should be considered at
elevated risk of further delay beyond their originally quoted ETA. Carrier
TransGlobal Freight has not provided a revised delivery commitment as of
this alert.
