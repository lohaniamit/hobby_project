# Procurement Comment - PO-9999001 Expedite Request

Submitted formal expedite request to NorthStar Sensor Systems for
PO-9999001 (600 units of SC-417, promised date 2026-08-20, destination
Plant P2). Supplier acknowledged the request on 2026-07-24 but could not
commit an accelerated ship date due to the semiconductor allocation issue
referenced in the July supplier risk note.

Shipment SHP-0021122 (see shipments.csv) was eventually released on
2026-07-25 via TransGlobal Freight but is now tracking delayed against its
original ETA of 2026-08-20; carrier cites congestion on the
Mexico-to-Plant-P2 truck corridor (see logistics_lanes.csv, lane
LANE-000966, elevated risk score for August 2026) in addition to the
supplier-side timing issue.

Buyer recommends Materials Planning not count this shipment's contents as
available supply for the September 11 build until an updated arrival
estimate is confirmed.
